How It Works

No hidden fees. No fine print. Here's exactly what happens from the moment you apply.

1

You Apply with Your Idea

Tell us your dream. A lighting company, a dental practice, a food truck, a tutoring service — any legitimate business idea. We review your application within 48 hours.

Your time: 15 minutes
2

We Research & Plan

Using AI-powered tools (Gemini, NotebookLM), we conduct deep market research, competitive analysis, and build a bank-ready business plan for your idea. We identify your target market, pricing strategy, and growth opportunities.

We deliver: Business plan — Value: $5,000–$10,000
3

We Build Your Brand

Professional logo, color palette, typography, brand mascot, marketing copy. A complete identity that looks like you hired a $15,000 design agency — because the technology makes it possible.

We deliver: Full brand identity — Value: $8,000–$15,000
4

We Build Your Website

6–8 page premium website with animations, mobile responsiveness, SEO, contact forms, and hosting. Not a template — a custom-designed, hand-crafted digital presence deployed live.

We deliver: Live website — Value: $10,000–$25,000
5

We Launch Your Merch Store

Branded t-shirts, hoodies, hats, polos — designed, connected to Stripe payment processing, and ready to sell. Zero inventory. Print-on-demand fulfillment. Revenue flows directly to you.

We deliver: E-commerce store — Value: $3,000–$5,000
6

You Execute. We Support.

You run your business — serve customers, build relationships, grow. We handle ongoing digital infrastructure, updates, and technical support. When you make money, we share in 10% of profits as our cooperative partnership.

You pay: $0 upfront — Only 10% of profits when profitable

Our Structure

The Hybrid Model

A nonprofit foundation backed by a for-profit technology company. Complete transparency on how the two work together.

Think! Ventures Foundation

501(c)(3) Nonprofit

  • ✓ Applies for grants and funding
  • ✓ Recruits and selects entrepreneurs
  • ✓ Runs workshops and programs
  • ✓ Measures and reports social impact
  • ✓ Issues tax-deductible donation receipts
  • ✓ Owns cooperative partnership agreements

Think! Design & Planning, LLC

For-Profit Technology Partner

  • ✓ Provides technology services at fair market rates
  • ✓ Builds websites, brands, and AI tools
  • ✓ Maintains infrastructure (hosting, APIs, MCP)
  • ✓ Owns proprietary tech (AVA, Gemini integrations)
  • ✓ Contracted by Foundation — disclosed in Form 990
  • ✓ Also serves direct (non-Foundation) clients
