Graduated Give-Back Model

Think! Ventures provides everything at zero upfront cost. Once your business is profitable, a small contribution funds the next wave of entrepreneurs.

  • Year 1: 10% of net profits (capped at $5,000)
  • Year 2: 7% of net profits (capped at $5,000)
  • Year 3: 5% of net profits (capped at $5,000)
  • Year 4+: Voluntary contributions -- pay it forward

Every dollar you give back helps launch another entrepreneur just like you.
