Think! Ventures Foundation -- Ready-Made Business Franchise Explainer Video Script

TARGET LENGTH: 3 to 4 minutes
TONE: Clear, exciting, accessible

HOOK (0:00 to 0:20)
What if you could own a business tomorrow? Not someday. Tomorrow. No franchise fee. No startup capital. No figuring it out alone. Just step in and start operating.

WHAT IS A READY-MADE BUSINESS? (0:20 to 1:00)
Think! Ventures Foundation builds complete, proven business models from the ground up. Full brand. Professional website. E-commerce and booking systems. Business plan with pricing strategy. Operations manual that tells you exactly how to deliver the service. Marketing materials. Everything.

Then we put it in a catalog. You browse it. You pick the business that fits you. You claim your city. And we hand you the keys to a business that's already built.

No franchise fee. No startup capital. Zero cost.

HOW IS THIS POSSIBLE? (1:00 to 1:30)
Think! Ventures is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. We build these businesses using grant funding. The same grants that fund job training and workforce development fund us -- because that's exactly what we do. We create jobs by creating business owners.

Your success is our grant report. Every operator we launch proves to funders that the model works. That unlocks bigger grants. That funds more business models. The cycle feeds itself.

HOW IT WORKS FOR YOU (1:30 to 2:30)
Step one: Browse the catalog. See what's available. Each listing shows the business, what it offers, what you need to get started, and which cities are open.

Step two: Apply. Tell us your name, your city, and why this business fits you. We review it and set up a discovery call.

Step three: We customize. In 1 to 2 days, we localize the website for your market, set up your Google Business Profile, create your social media accounts, and hand you the operations manual.

Step four: You launch. With a 12-month coaching program. Monthly check-ins. Business communication workshops. You learn the systems, you learn the service, you learn how to grow.

Step five: You operate. You run the business. You keep 90 percent of your profits. 10 percent goes back to the Foundation to fund the next entrepreneur.

THE FIRST READY-MADE BUSINESS (2:30 to 3:15)
Our first Ready-Made Business is Arlan -- premium exterior lighting and home services. Holiday lighting. Permanent LED systems. Landscape lighting.

Arlan Greensboro is already live and operating. The brand is proven. The website is built. The pricing is set. The operations manual is written.

Now imagine Arlan Durham. Arlan Raleigh. Arlan Charlotte. Arlan Atlanta. Same brand. Same systems. Same quality. Different operator. Different city.

You don't need experience in exterior lighting. You need a truck, a ladder, and the willingness to learn. We teach you everything else.

CALL TO ACTION (3:15 to 3:45)
This is not a franchise. There are no fees. There are no royalties on revenue. You pay nothing until you're profitable -- and even then, it's just 10 percent of net profits going back to help the next person.

Browse our catalog at thinkventures.app. Find your business. Claim your market.

From dream to launch in days. Think! Ventures.

KEY DIFFERENCES FROM TRADITIONAL FRANCHISE:
Traditional franchise costs 50 thousand to 500 thousand dollars upfront. Think Ventures Ready-Made costs zero.
Traditional franchise charges 5 to 8 percent royalty on gross revenue. Think Ventures charges 10 percent only on net profit, only when profitable.
Traditional franchise is profit-driven. Think Ventures is mission-driven, funded by grants.
Traditional franchise has strict corporate control. Think Ventures has collaborative, supportive partnership.

WHO OWNS WHAT:
The brand is owned by Think Ventures Foundation (built with grant money).
The technology is maintained by Think Design and Planning LLC.
Customer relationships belong to the operator.
Revenue: 90 percent goes to the operator, 10 percent to the Foundation.
Each operator gets an exclusive geographic territory. One operator per city. Protected market.
